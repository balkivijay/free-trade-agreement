Reading this page

Figures with a full percentage-to-percentage chart come from named tariff numbers in current reporting. Cards marked timing TBC report a direction of travel without an official schedule — treat those as provisional until the EU–India legal texts are published. Country relevance is illustrative: the EU negotiates tariffs as one bloc, so rates don't vary by member state — the country view highlights where each economy's own export strengths overlap with the deal.
